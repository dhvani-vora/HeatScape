# HeatScape 

### Urban Heat Reduction Planner

HeatScape is a data-driven platform that identifies urban heat hotspots, analyzes their likely causes, and recommends targeted cooling interventions with estimated environmental impact and implementation cost.

## Problem

Urban heat is not distributed evenly across a city. Areas with high built-up density, limited vegetation, extensive impervious surfaces, and dense road networks can experience significantly higher heat exposure.

However, identifying a hotspot is only the first step. Cities need to know:

- Where are the hottest areas?
- What factors are contributing to the heat?
- Which interventions would be most effective?
- How much would those interventions cost?

## Our Solution

HeatScape combines urban environmental data with AI-assisted analysis to:

1. Detect urban heat hotspots
2. Identify likely contributing factors
3. Recommend targeted cooling interventions
4. Estimate potential cooling impact
5. Estimate implementation cost
6. Help prioritize interventions
