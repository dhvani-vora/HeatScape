# Cooling Interventions

HeatScape will evaluate interventions based on the characteristics of each hotspot.

## Planned Interventions

### Tree Canopy Expansion
Best suited for:
- Low vegetation
- High pedestrian exposure
- Available open/public space

Potential benefits:
- Shade
- Reduced surface temperature
- Improved thermal comfort

### Cool Roofs
Best suited for:
- High building density
- Large roof coverage
- Low existing roof reflectivity

Potential benefits:
- Reduced heat absorption
- Lower building surface temperatures

### Permeable / Cool Pavement
Best suited for:
- High impervious surface
- Large paved areas
- High road/parking density

Potential benefits:
- Reduced surface heat
- Improved stormwater management

### Shade Structures
Best suited for:
- High pedestrian density
- Transit areas
- Public spaces

Potential benefits:
- Improved pedestrian thermal comfort

### Green Corridors
Best suited for:
- Areas with fragmented vegetation
- Major roads
- Connected public/open spaces

Potential benefits:
- Increased vegetation
- Shade
- Improved urban environmental conditions
